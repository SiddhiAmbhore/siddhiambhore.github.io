I work in combinatorial commutative algebra, studying how a graph governs the invariants of its ideals. I completed my Ph.D. at IIT Gandhinagar in 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Indranath Sengupta, as a Prime Minister's Research Fellow, and I continue to work with him as a postdoctoral Research Associate.

Research

Monomial ideals

Associated primes as colon ideals

I described each associated prime of a monomial ideal explicitly as a colon ideal, using the irredundant irreducible decomposition, and implemented the method in Macaulay2.

Binomial edge ideals

The v-number

In joint work, I initiated the study of the v-number of binomial edge ideals, relating it to completion sets, domination numbers and Castelnuovo–Mumford regularity, and classified the graphs whose binomial edge ideals have v-number one.

I use Macaulay2, Singular, GAP and Python to test conjectures. Ph.D. thesis: Associated Primes of Monomial Ideals and the v-Number of Binomial Edge Ideals (IIT Gandhinagar, 2026).
